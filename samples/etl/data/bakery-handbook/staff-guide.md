# Staff guide

Everything in this handbook is made up for the Cuttlely demo.

## Daily routine

1. The opening baker starts at 5:00 and bakes bread first, then pastry.
2. Shelves are counted at 11:00 and 14:00. Anything below four items gets a second bake if there is time.
3. Bread left at closing goes to the Harbor Street food bank the same evening. Pastry left at closing is sold half price the next morning at Riverside only.

## Prices and offers

-   Prices are the same in both shops.
-   Loyalty card: the tenth coffee or pastry is free.
-   Orders over twenty items need two days' notice and a 20% deposit.

## Who to ask

-   Ordering and suppliers: the Riverside head baker.
-   Shop rota and holidays: the Old Town shop lead.
