# Allergens and ingredients

Everything in this handbook is made up for the Cuttlely demo.

Both shops bake in kitchens that handle wheat, rye, milk, eggs, sesame and tree nuts. Staff cannot promise that any item is free of these.

## Item by item

-   **Sourdough Loaf** and **Baguette**: wheat. No milk or eggs.
-   **Seeded Rye**: rye, wheat and sesame.
-   **Croissant**: wheat, milk (butter) and eggs (glaze).
-   **Cinnamon Roll**: wheat, milk and eggs. The icing contains milk.
-   **Blueberry Muffin**: wheat, milk and eggs.
-   **Chocolate Chip Cookie**: wheat, milk, eggs and soy (chocolate).
-   **Lemon Tart**: wheat, milk and eggs. The base is made with ground almonds, a tree nut.

## Gluten-free

There is no gluten-free range. When asked, staff point people to the ingredient list above and never guess.
