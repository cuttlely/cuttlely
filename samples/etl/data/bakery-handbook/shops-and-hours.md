# Shops and opening hours

Everything in this handbook is made up for the Cuttlely demo.

## Riverside

Harbor Street 12. The bigger of the two shops, with twelve seats by the window.

-   Monday to Friday: 7:00 to 18:00
-   Saturday: 7:30 to 16:00
-   Sunday: closed

The first sourdough comes out of the oven at 6:30, so the shelves are full at opening.

## Old Town

Mill Lane 3. A small counter shop, takeaway only.

-   Tuesday to Saturday: 8:00 to 15:00
-   Sunday: 9:00 to 13:00
-   Monday: closed

Old Town sells out of croissants most Saturdays by 11:00. Customers can reserve up to a dozen by phone the day before.
