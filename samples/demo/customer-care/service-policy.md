# Larkfield Home Service Recovery Policy

Larkfield Home is a made-up store. Everything in this policy is made up for the Cuttlely demo. Only the policy specialist reads this file.

## Part 1: whose fault a late delivery is

### 1.1 Late

An order is late when it has not been delivered by the promised date on the order.

### 1.2 Larkfield fault

The delay is Larkfield's fault when it starts before the carrier's first pickup scan. That covers picking and packing mistakes, stock errors, and a parcel held at a warehouse check. A parcel whose label was created but that was not handed to the carrier within one business day is a Larkfield fault. Tracking that shows only "Label created" means the carrier never had the parcel, so it is never a carrier delay.

### 1.3 Carrier fault

The delay is the carrier's fault when it starts after the carrier's pickup scan: a hub delay, weather, a road closure, a missed delivery scan, or a parcel lost in transit.

### 1.4 Customer fault

The delay is the customer's fault when the address was wrong or a signature delivery was refused. Offer to resend at the customer's cost.

### 1.5 Deciding fault

Decide fault from the warehouse scans and the carrier scans, never from what the customer reads on the tracking page. When the records disagree, the warehouse scans win.

## Part 2: remedies

### 2.1 Larkfield-fault remedy

For a Larkfield-fault late delivery, do all of these:

-   Refund the shipping the customer paid.
-   Reship the item today at Larkfield's cost, by the fastest service, from the warehouse with stock that delivers first.
-   Give a store credit by tier, from the table in 2.3.
-   Recall the held parcel so it is not shipped twice.

### 2.2 Carrier-fault remedy

For a carrier-fault late delivery, open a trace with the carrier the same day and give a goodwill credit by tier, from the table in 2.4. Refund the shipping only for Plus and Premier customers. Reship only when the carrier has not scanned the parcel for 3 business days or declares it lost.

### 2.3 Larkfield-fault credit by tier

| Tier     | Store credit                                                  |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Standard | 10% of the item price                                         |
| Plus     | 15% of the item price                                         |
| Premier  | 20% of the item price, and a call from the Customer Care Lead |

The credit is at most $75 per order.

### 2.4 Goodwill credit by tier

| Tier     | Goodwill credit |
| -------- | --------------- |
| Standard | $10             |
| Plus     | $15             |
| Premier  | $25             |

A customer gets at most one goodwill credit in any 90 days.

### 2.5 Larkfield-fault credit is not goodwill

A Larkfield-fault credit (2.3) is not a goodwill credit. It does not count toward the 90-day goodwill limit, and an earlier goodwill credit never reduces it.

## Part 3: repeat problems, refunds and escalation

### 3.1 Repeat late delivery

When the customer had another late delivery, for any reason, in the 120 days before this complaint, give the remedy one tier higher: Standard gets the Plus remedy and Plus gets the Premier remedy. A Premier customer keeps the Premier remedy, with the credit cap raised to $100.

### 3.2 Refund requests

When the customer asks for a refund and a reship can arrive within 3 business days, offer both and let the customer choose: the reship with the full remedy, or a full refund of the order. Never refuse a refund for a Larkfield-fault late delivery.

### 3.3 Escalation

Escalate to the Customer Care Lead when a Premier remedy applies. Open an operations incident when the same warehouse problem held more than 3 orders, and name the packing wave.

### 3.4 The reply to the customer

Say plainly what happened and whose fault it was. Never blame the carrier for a Larkfield-fault delay. Give the date the reship will arrive. Do not promise anything this policy does not allow.
